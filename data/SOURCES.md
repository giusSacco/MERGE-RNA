# Data sources

## Mutation-rate data

Sequencing data were collected from the following sources and then processed to obtain mutation rates with a [RNAframework](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/46/16/e97/5035169?guestAccessKey=)-based pipeline as described in the manuscript. Please see the original publications for details and cite the original works if you use the data in your work.

| Directory | Source |
|---|---|
| `structured_rnas/` | [Bohn et al. 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-023-01862-7) |
| `adenine_riboswitch/` | [Olson et al. 2023](https://www.cell.com/molecular-cell/abstract/S1097-2765(22)00114-9) |
| `cspA/` | [Zhang et al. 2018](https://www.cell.com/molecular-cell/abstract/S1097-2765(18)30180-1) |
| `designed_sequence/` | [Sacco et al. 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20581) |


## structured_rnas/\<system\>/reference/ — reference 3D structures

PDB models for hc16, tetrahymena_ribozyme, and V_chol_gly_riboswitch:

[Kappel et al. 2020](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-020-0878-9)

Dot-bracket secondary-structure annotations generated with Barnaba:

[Bottaro et al. 2018](https://rnajournal.cshlp.org/content/25/2/219.short)

The HCV IRES sequence and dot-bracket secondary structure (`HCV_IRES/reference/ires`)
were transcribed from Extended Data Fig. 6 of Bohn et al. 2023 (see above).
